## 🔢 Gratuity Formula (MOHRE 2026)

```
First 5 Years:   (Basic Salary ÷ 30) × 21 days × Years
After 5 Years:   (Basic Salary ÷ 30) × 30 days × Years
Maximum Cap:     Basic Salary × 24 months
```

### Example:
- Basic Salary: **AED 5,000**
- Service: **3 years**
- Gratuity = (5000 ÷ 30) × 21 × 3 = **AED 10,500**

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Enter your **Basic Monthly Salary** (AED)
2. Select **Contract Type** (Limited / Unlimited)
3. Select **Reason for Leaving** (Resignation / Termination)
4. Enter **Start Date** and **End Date**
5. Click **"Calculate Gratuity"**
6. Get instant results with full breakdown ✅

## 📋 UAE Gratuity Rules

| Service Period | Days Per Year |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | No gratuity |
| 1 - 5 years | 21 days/year |
| More than 5 years | 30 days/year |
| Maximum cap | 2 years salary |
